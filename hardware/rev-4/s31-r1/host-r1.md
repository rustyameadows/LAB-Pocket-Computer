# ESP32-S31 host and radio, revision 1

Both display variants use ESP32-S31NRV16 with 16 MB in-package octal PSRAM, 16 MB W25Q128JVSIQ NOR and the retained 40 MHz ABM8 crystal. Host sheets use actual package pad numbers. The GPIO map uses all 54 general-purpose signals available after reserving the 6 NOR pins. The dedicated HS USB pads and EN are outside that count. The 3.4-inch display uses 9-bit commands on the I²C expander for initialization. The 4-inch panel uses its module defaults. Both send pixels on a hardware RGB565 bus. [Espressif hardware design guide, tables 11–12](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-hardware-design-guidelines/en/latest/esp32s31/schematic-checklist.html)

| Interface | ESP32-S31 GPIO | Function |
|---|---|---|
| RGB data | 8–12, 13–18, 19/33/34/35/36 | Blue bits 0–4, green bits 0–5, red bits 0–4, least significant first |

| Backlight | 42 | Hardware LEDC PWM, both displays |
| Camera | 46–53, 54, 55, 56, 57 | D0–D7, PCLK, XCLK, VSYNC, HREF |
| SCCB and system I²C | 2, 3 | SDA, SCL. U4 is at 0x20, U401 at 0x21, OV5640 at 0x3c |
| Microphones | 0, 1, 4 | PDM_A, PDM_B, clock. I²S0 supports four RX data lines. LAB uses two, one clock and two opposite-edge mics per line |

At power-up U4 and U401 are inputs with weak pull-ups. Firmware loads safe output latches before changing direction. On U401, bank 0 bits 0 through 7 are LCD CS, LCD SCK, LCD SDI, LCD reset, camera reset, camera PWDN, camera analog enable and RP RUN. Bank 1 bits 0 and 1 read the removable and internal card switches. U4 retains CAM_ENABLE. The display module’s flex data inputs must stay high impedance through the GPIO36 flash-voltage sampling interval. R403 holds GPIO36 high. Both socket selects have pull-ups, which also establish GPIO37 and GPIO60 strap states. Test cold boot and reset with both sockets loaded. [TI PCA9555](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/pca9555.pdf) [ESP32-S31 series data sheet, chapter 3](https://documentation.espressif.com/esp32-s31_datasheet_en.pdf)

The dedicated receiver can sample the native camera and the independent display module runs native RGB. Bring-up should exercise simultaneous camera capture, display refresh at the appropriate pixel clock and audio. The S31 SDK reports support for up to four hardware PDM RX lanes and a PDM-to-PCM filter on I²S0. The codec can run concurrently on I²S1. The shared SPI bus still requires firmware to manage three independent chip selects. The RP2350B retains its SWD recovery pads. A USB update workflow for RP over S31 requires installed bridge/update firmware. [Espressif SoC capabilities](https://github.com/espressif/esp-idf/blob/master/components/soc/esp32s31/include/soc/soc_caps.h)

## Power and layout

S31 VDDA and VDDPST rails require 3.0 to 3.6 V at the package. Espressif specifies a source rated for at least 600 mA. The 2 nH L401 has 35 mΩ maximum DCR, for 21 mV drop at a conservative 600 mA on that branch. The chip's 1.8 V LDO output powers its integrated PSRAM. The 3.3 V mode of the flash rail passes the system voltage through a switch with 3 Ω typical resistance. The W25Q128JV accepts 2.7 to 3.6 V and draws up to 25 mA, corresponding to a typical 75 mV switch drop at that current. [ESP32-S31 datasheet, tables 5-2 and 5-3](https://documentation.espressif.com/esp32-s31_datasheet_en.pdf) [Coilcraft 0402DC-2N0](https://www.coilcraft.com/en-us/products/rf/ceramic-core-chip-inductors/0402-%281005%29/0402dc/0402dc-2n0/)

The shared system rail is constrained by the RP2350B’s 3.135 V VREG_AVDD and USB_OTP_VDD minima and the modules’ 3.30 V recommended maximum. The RP’s R61/C110 filter is 33 Ω / 4.7 µF. Raspberry Pi reports about 200 µA on that pin, about 6.6 mV across the resistor. Measure both sides of R61 and the panel VCI throughout regulator startup, battery brownout, radio transmission, peak display, LED and speaker load, and load release. Forced-PWM static feedback tolerances do not include these transients. [Raspberry Pi RP2350 data sheet, table 1688 and §6.1.4](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/rp2350/rp2350-datasheet.pdf) [Hardware Design with RP2350](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/rp2350/hardware-design-with-rp2350.pdf) [TI TPS63070](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps63070.pdf)

Place U1, U9, Y1 and local bypass components close to their corresponding power/signal pins. Keep C413–C417 adjacent to U1’s PSRAM supply pads and clock-loop components separated from RF and high-current switching traces. C422/C423 are 15 pF starting loads for the 10 pF crystal. Frequency and 24 nH inductor tuning require a populated board. Use short length-matched DVP/RGB buses, a continuous reference under the buses and USB, and 90 Ω differential USB on the selected stackup. [Espressif PCB layout guide](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-hardware-design-guidelines/en/latest/esp32s31/pcb-layout-design.html)

ANT401 is an onboard 2.4 GHz ceramic antenna. Its footprint reserves a 6.5 × 6.5 mm corner and provides two 0.8 × 1.6 mm solder lands with 2.6 mm inner spacing. In its zero-rotation local coordinate system the board corner is at x = +2.5 mm, y = −1.7 mm. Keep ground copper, other components, frame, pack and speaker metal out of that volume. Feed the antenna from its marked end. C426, L403 and C427 are the first chip-end match. The 0201 R416 series link and unpopulated L406 shunt provide the two additional matching positions from Espressif’s CLCCL drawing. Route 50 Ω GCPW into the separate Johanson series/shunt/series antenna match. Prototype values must be measured and tuned with the display, bonded battery and cables installed. Correct stub placement, final RF match and radiated behavior require a routed board and measurements. [Johanson mounting and matching drawing, page 3](https://www.johansontechnology.com/docs/3827/Antenna-2450AT18A0100001E-Rev4.0.pdf)
