# LAB Pocket Rev 4 · S31 panel studies · R1

The starting point is the routed Rev 3A at commit 9af14bc. Both projects use the bare ESP32-S31NRV16 and the Seeed Studio 114993115 autofocus OV5640 flex camera. The 4-inch project uses Adafruit 5795 (TL040HDS20-B1502A, 720 × 720). The 3.4-inch project uses Adafruit 5825 (TL034WVS05-B1477A, 480 × 480). Both panels are non-touch.

The battery remains the bonded Jauch 246524. The carrier retains the RP2350B user processor, four PDM mics, NAU88C22 codec, stereo amplifier, 20 keys and their local LEDs, 12 perimeter LEDs, dual microSD, the side audio jack, and bottom USB-C. The S31 supplies the radio function. The new parallel camera and display interfaces replace the P4-era host interfaces.

The deliverable is an editable electrical and mechanical study ahead of prototype spend. Routed P4 copper and manufacturing files belong to Rev 3A.
